# SaaS Company – Financial Performance Dashboard
**FP20 Analytics Challenge 25 | Power BI | DAX | ZoomCharts**

### Project Overview
A financial performance analysis of a SaaS company covering 2022–2024. The project explores revenue, expenses, profitability, budget performance and financial trends through interactive Power BI dashboards.

### Objective
To evaluate the company's financial performance, identify major revenue and expense drivers, assess actual performance against budget and uncover trends that can support financial planning and business decisions.

### My Contribution
Participated in FP20 Analytics Challenge 25, developing an interactive financial dashboard to analyse three years of company financial data. My work focused on financial performance analysis, budget variance assessment, KPI reporting and data visualisation.

### Tools & Process

- **Data Analysis:** Examined financial transactions, revenue, expenses, budgets and account classifications.
- **Financial Modelling:** Analysed actual versus budget performance, profitability, revenue composition and expense distribution.
- **DAX:** Developed financial measures and calculations for KPIs, variances and year-over-year comparisons.
- **Visualisation:** Built interactive dashboards using Power BI and ZoomCharts, incorporating financial matrices, trend charts, category breakdowns and slicers.

### Key Insights
**1. Overall Budget Performance**
Across 2022–2024, the analysis reports cumulative spending approximately $511.9K below plan. Annual variance figures highlight differences in budget performance across the three years.

**2. Profitability Trends**

- 2022 recorded a net loss of $385.2K.
- 2023 recorded a net loss of approximately $1.36M.
- 2024 recorded a net profit of approximately $299.9K.

**3. Revenue Performance**
SaaS subscriptions generated approximately $14.29M, representing 79.6% of total revenue, while product revenue contributed approximately $3.61M (20.1%). Monthly revenue increased across the three-year period.

**4. Expense Drivers**
Payroll was identified as a major expense category, with approximately $12.24M recorded. ADP was the largest vendor outflow, at approximately $10.61M, primarily associated with payroll.

**5. Seasonality**
The analysis identified monthly fluctuations in revenue and expenses, with revenue strengthening during parts of the year and expenses showing notable increases around July and August.

### Business Recommendations

- Strengthen budget monitoring and investigate significant monthly spending variances.
- Maintain focus on SaaS revenue growth while monitoring the contribution of product revenue.
- Review major expense categories, particularly payroll and cost of goods sold.
- Use historical monthly trends to improve financial forecasting and budget allocation.

This challenge strengthened my practical understanding of financial performance analysis, budget variance calculations, profitability reporting, DAX and interactive dashboard development using Power BI and ZoomCharts.
