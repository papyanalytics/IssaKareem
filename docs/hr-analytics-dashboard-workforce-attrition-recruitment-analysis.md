# HR Analytics Dashboard – Workforce, Attrition & Recruitment Analysis
**ZoomCharts HR Analytics Challenge | Power BI | DAX | ZoomCharts**

### Project Overview
A three-page HR analytics dashboard developed to examine workforce composition, employee costs, attrition, retention, engagement and recruitment performance. The project transforms HR metrics into interactive insights to support workforce planning and people-related decisions.

### Objective
To evaluate employee turnover, monitor salary costs against budget, identify workforce trends and examine recruitment and retention indicators across departments.

### My Contribution
Completed the project as part of the ZoomCharts HR Analytics Challenge, despite missing the submission deadline. Developed the three-page Power BI dashboard, created analytical measures using DAX and designed interactive reports using ZoomCharts visualisations.

### Tools & Process

- **Power BI:** Developed three interconnected analytical report pages.
- **DAX:** Created measures for headcount, FTE, salary variance, attrition, termination, retention and recruitment KPIs.
- **Data Analysis:** Examined workforce costs, employee exits, tenure, engagement and recruitment performance.
- **ZoomCharts:** Used interactive visualisations to explore departmental performance, workforce trends and employee-related metrics.

### Dashboard Analysis
**1. Executive Overview**
Examines workforce size, FTE utilisation, salary expenditure, budget variance and employee terminations across departments.

**2. Attrition & Termination Analysis**
Explores voluntary and involuntary exits, attrition rates, employee tenure, termination reasons and geographical patterns.

**3. Retention Drivers & Recruitment**
Examines employee engagement, high-performer distribution, recruitment fill rates, time-to-fill and absence patterns.

### Key Insights

- **Workforce:** 999 employees and 969 FTE, with FTE utilisation of 96.96%.
- **Compensation:** Actual salary expenditure of approximately $96M against a $71M budget, with a reported salary cost variance of 35.26%.
- **Employee Attrition:** Overall attrition rate of 14.29%, comprising 12.84% involuntary and 3.65% voluntary attrition.
- **Termination Drivers:** Company restructuring accounted for 65.97% of recorded terminations.
- **Recruitment & Retention:** The dashboard reports an 86% retention rate, 56% recruitment fill rate and average time-to-fill of 48 days.

### Recommendations

- Investigate salary expenditure exceeding budget and identify the departments driving the variance.
- Examine restructuring-related employee exits and their impact on workforce stability.
- Strengthen retention initiatives by monitoring engagement and attrition patterns.
- Improve recruitment efficiency by tracking open vacancies, fill rates and time-to-fill.
