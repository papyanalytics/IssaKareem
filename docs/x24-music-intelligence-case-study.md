# X24 Music Intelligence – Album Performance & Market Analysis
**Independent Research Project | Power BI | Music Analytics | Data Research**

### Project Overview
An independent music intelligence project focused on analysing Davido's *5IVE* album performance across streaming platforms and international music charts. I independently sourced and organised music performance data to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard examining streaming trends, chart rankings, global reach and album rollout strategy.

### Objective
To evaluate the commercial performance of 5IVE, identify its strongest-performing tracks and platforms, examine its international chart presence and understand how promotional activities aligned with its performance.

### My Contribution
Independently researched and collected music performance information from streaming platforms, official music channels and chart-tracking sources. Structured the collected information into analytical datasets, developed the Power BI dashboard and translated the findings into performance insights.

### Tools & Process

- **Data Research:** Collected Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, iTunes and Billboard performance information.
- **Data Organisation:** Structured track-level, platform-level, chart-ranking, country and marketing timeline information for analysis.
- **Power BI:** Developed interactive dashboards with KPIs, ranking tables, streaming comparisons, trend visualisations and filters.
- **Business Intelligence:** Examined platform contribution, track performance, international reach and promotional rollout patterns.

### Key Insights

- **Streaming Performance:** The dashboard reports approximately 414M Spotify streams and 181M YouTube views.
- **Track Performance:** *With You* leads the Spotify track ranking with approximately 127M streams.
- **Platform Contribution:** Spotify accounts for approximately 69.96% of the combined streaming and video-view metric displayed in the dashboard.
- **International Reach:** The analysis records chart placements across five countries.
- **Marketing Rollout:** The dashboard examines a 169-day promotional timeline leading to the album's release on 18 April 2025.

### Project Extension
Extended the research to compare 5IVE's established performance with the early rollout of Davido's 2026 album, *ORIADÉ*. The comparison focuses on streaming momentum, promotional singles and early audience engagement rather than treating an ongoing release as a completed album performance.

### Project Outcome
Developed a multi-page music intelligence dashboard combining independently sourced music data, chart research and interactive visualisation to demonstrate how entertainment industry data can support performance analysis and strategic insights.
