# Pharmacy Sales & Profitability Analytics
**Power BI | DAX | ZoomCharts | Sales & Financial Analysis**

### Project Overview
An interactive pharmaceutical distribution analytics dashboard developed to evaluate sales performance, product profitability and geographic contribution across European markets. The analysis examines revenue, margins, units sold, promotional efficiency and sales trends to support commercial decision-making.

### Objective
To identify the products, countries and pharmacy segments driving revenue and profitability, evaluate sales trends and assess how promotional activities affect profit margins.

### My Contribution
Designed and developed a multi-page Power BI dashboard, combining financial KPIs, product-level analysis, geographic visualisation and time-based performance comparisons. Translated sales data into business insights and recommendations for product optimisation and market performance.

### Tools & Process

- **Power BI:** Developed the interactive dashboard and analytical report pages.
- **DAX:** Used measures to evaluate revenue, total margin, costs, units sold, growth and margin percentages.
- **Data Analysis:** Examined product categories, brands, pharmacy performance, country contributions and promotional effectiveness.
- **ZoomCharts:** Incorporated interactive visualisations for geographic analysis, product profitability and performance trends.

### Dashboard Analysis
**1. Executive Summary:** Provides an overview of revenue, margin, costs, units sold, growth and country-level revenue contribution.

**2. Product Performance:** Examines product category revenue, units sold, margin contribution and product-level profitability.

**3. Geographic Performance:** Compares country-level margins, revenue concentration, pharmacy segments and geographic performance.

**4. Time & Trend Performance:** Analyses monthly revenue, margin and unit trends, rolling revenue momentum and seasonal performance patterns.

### Key Insights

- **Overall Performance:** Generated dashboard-reported revenue of approximately €8.63M, total margin of €2.42M and 445.79K units sold.
- **Profitability:** Recorded an average margin of 28.0%.
- **Geographic Contribution:** Germany led country revenue contribution at approximately €1.57M, followed by France at €1.41M and Italy at €1.33M.
- **Product Performance:** Prescription products generated the largest category revenue contribution, at approximately €2.8M.
- **Promotional Efficiency:** Promoted products recorded a 19.9% margin compared with 29.0% for non-promoted products, indicating lower margin efficiency among promoted products.

### Business Recommendations

- Review promotional pricing and discount strategies to improve margin efficiency.
- Prioritise high-performing products while monitoring lower-margin product categories.
- Evaluate opportunities for growth in countries with strong revenue and profitability performance.
- Use seasonal trends to improve inventory planning and sales forecasting.
