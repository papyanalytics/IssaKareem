# Cafe Sales Performance Analysis
**Real-World Analytics Engagement | Python | Jupyter Notebook | Power BI**

### Project Overview
A real-world cafe sales analytics project undertaken following a request from another analyst seeking support with data analysis. The project involved transforming transactional sales data into meaningful business insights using Python and Power BI.

### Business Objective
To evaluate sales performance, identify high-performing products, examine monthly revenue trends and understand how sales channels and payment methods contribute to overall business performance.

### My Role & Contribution
Engaged to apply my data analytics skills to the cafe sales dataset. I handled data inspection, cleaning and exploratory analysis using Python, followed by the development of an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate key performance indicators and business insights.

### Tools & Analytical Process

- **Python & Pandas:** Inspected 10,000 transaction records and assessed data quality.
- **Data Cleaning:** Converted data types, handled missing values, standardised unspecified categories and filtered incomplete records.
- **Feature Engineering:** Extracted year, month number and month name from transaction dates.
- **Jupyter Notebook:** Conducted data preparation and exploratory analysis.
- **Power BI:** Developed interactive visualisations covering revenue, transactions, product performance, monthly trends and sales channels.

### Key Insights

- **Sales Performance:** The dashboard reports approximately ₦88.8K revenue, 10K transactions and 30.1K units sold.
- **Product Performance:** Salad recorded the highest displayed product revenue at approximately ₦17K, followed by Sandwich and Smoothie.
- **Revenue Trends:** July recorded a noticeable revenue spike in the monthly trend.
- **Channel Performance:** The "Not Specified" location category contributed approximately ₦35.3K in revenue, highlighting a data classification improvement opportunity.
- **Monthly Growth:** The dashboard reports 8.8% monthly growth.

### Business Recommendations

- Prioritise high-performing products and monitor changing customer demand.
- Investigate the July revenue spike to understand its contributing factors.
- Improve transaction data capture to reduce unspecified sales-channel and location records.
- Use sales trends to support inventory planning and operational decisions.

### Project Outcome
Delivered a structured sales analysis and interactive Power BI dashboard that makes key business performance indicators easier to monitor and explore.
